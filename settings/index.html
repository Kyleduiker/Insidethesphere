Comparable adjustments

What each difference between a comparable and the subject property is worth. These are set once and apply to every CMA — they are your valuation method, not something re-decided per listing. They never reach the client page. A seller sees the adjusted price and the reason for it, never the rate that produced it.

The sign is handled for you. An adjustment always moves the comparable toward the subject. If the comp has three bedrooms above grade and yours has two, the comp is adjusted down by one bedroom. If yours has more, the comp is adjusted up. You never enter a negative number here — enter what the feature is worth, and leave the direction to the arithmetic.
Per-unit rates
Multiplied by the difference between the comparable and the subject. Leave a rate at zero to switch that adjustment off entirely — it will not be applied and will not appear in the breakdown.
