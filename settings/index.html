Comparable adjustments

What each difference between a comparable and the subject property is worth. These are set once and apply to every CMA — they are your valuation method, not something re-decided per listing. These values are never published. Adjustments are worked out when you publish a CMA, and only the result for each comparable is written to the client page — this grid is not. Bear in mind that a line a seller does see can be divided by its difference: "1 more bedroom, − $15,000" shows the bedroom rate that CMA used.

The sign is handled for you. An adjustment always moves the comparable toward the subject. If the comp has three bedrooms above grade and yours has two, the comp is adjusted down by one bedroom. If yours has more, the comp is adjusted up. You never enter a negative number here — enter what the feature is worth, and leave the direction to the arithmetic.
Per-unit rates
Multiplied by the difference between the comparable and the subject. The default is what every comparable uses. Min and max set how far you can slide that line on an individual comp, when a feature there is worth more or less than usual — leave both blank and the line uses the default with no slider. Blank and zero are not the same thing. A blank default means you have not decided yet, and this page will tell you so. A default of zero means you have decided not to adjust on it — that factor is skipped silently and never appears in a breakdown.

Age
A value per age band rather than a rate per year, because a year of age is not worth the same at every age. The adjustment is the gap between the subject's band and the comparable's. Two homes in the same band are not adjusted, and neither are two built less than 5 years apart, even across a band edge. Age is measured as of the CMA's appointment date, never today. Renovation is not captured here — use the override on the comparable, with a reason.
Basement
What each state is worth, not a single "finished basement" figure. The adjustment is the difference between the two values, so a partially finished comparable against a fully finished subject adjusts by the gap rather than by all or nothing.
Garage
Valued the same way — a figure per type rather than a rate per bay, so a detached double need not be worth what an attached double is. Options match the garage list on the comparable and property forms exactly.
Applies to every CMA from the next time its comparables are viewed. Existing adjusted prices are recomputed, never stored.
